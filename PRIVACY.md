# Kanamachi: Privacy policy

Last updated: (date of publishing)

Kanamachi is a game for children and adults. It is made to collect nothing about you.

**What we collect:** nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It never asks for your
age, email, location, contacts or photos.

**What stays on your phone:** your progress and settings, and the nickname you give the game, are saved only on your device. If you
choose to copy a backup code, that code stays with you; we never receive it.

**Tester diary:** the game can keep an optional diary of how your matches went (scores, round times), with no names or personal
details. It stays on your phone and leaves it only if you choose to copy or share it yourself.

**Internet:** the game does not need the internet to play. Google Play may update the app as it does any other app.

**Notifications:** only if you turn them on (the game asks once, and there is a switch in Settings). They are made on your phone,
never at night. Nothing about you is sent anywhere for them.

**Children:** we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
